WaSupp privacy policy

Last updated : 25 November 2026 · version 1.5

In short

WaSupp has no server and no account. What you enter (profile, cupboard, medication, allergies, experiments) stays on your phone: the publisher never has access to it.

Some features contact public or open services. This page says which ones, when, and what they receive.

Without WaSupp+, the app shows ads from Google (AdMob). Google never receives your profile or your health data. In Europe, you first choose whether your data may be used to personalise ads.

A message sent with “Report a problem” reaches the publisher: what you write, the product concerned and, if you attach them, the technical details.

1. Who is responsible?

The publisher of WaSupp, named in the Legal notice. For any question about your data, write to the contact address.

2. Data kept on your phone

Profile: country, age, sex, weight, goals, sports, diet, allergies, anti-doping tests.

Weight tracking: your weight entries and their date.

Health: medicine families ticked in “My medication”, workouts, experiments (sleep, energy).

“My medications” saves nothing without your explicit consent. You can withdraw it at any time: your medications are then erased.

Use: cupboard, favourites, scan history, reminders, tubs, products viewed, label photos.

Apple Health, if you connect it: weight, workouts, sleep and protein eaten during the day, read only. The app writes nothing to Apple Health.

This data is only used to run the app for you: daily needs, alerts (allergens, overdose, interactions), reminders. It is never sent to the publisher, sold or used for advertising.

A label photo is read on the phone. It is never sent.

It may be copied into your phone's backup (iCloud or Google) if you have turned it on. That backup is managed by Apple or Google, under your account.

As with any website, each service receives your connection's IP address with the request. The app adds no identifier, except for ads (see “Advertising” below).

United States recalls (openFDA) are included in the app: no request is sent to the FDA.

4. Advertising

Without WaSupp+, the app shows ads: when you leave the page of a scanned product, at the bottom of the home screen, and when you choose to watch an ad to unlock an icon. They are provided by Google AdMob. With WaSupp+, there are no ads and AdMob is not started.

What Google receives: the IP address, the phone's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking), technical information (model, system, language, app version) and how ads are shown and tapped. Google uses this to choose, show and measure ads, and to fight fraud.

What Google never receives: your profile, your supplements, your allergies, your treatments, or any health data. Ads for food supplements, medicines or diets are blocked.

In Europe, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google form asks for your consent before any personalised ad. You can refuse: you will then see non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your mind at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.

On iPhone, Apple asks you whether the app may track you. If you refuse, the advertising identifier is not shared. On Android, you can delete or reset this identifier in the phone's settings.

Ads have no effect on scores or rankings.

The purchase of WaSupp+ is paid through the App Store or Google Play. The publisher receives no payment data.

Google processes this data under its own rules: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Permissions

Camera: read a barcode, photograph a label. Images stay on the phone.

Location “while using the app”: nearby shops only.

Notifications: intake reminders and alerts, scheduled on the phone.

Photos: save an image you choose to share.

Apple Health: read only, if you turn it on.

Tracking (iPhone): requested for personalised ads.

All are optional and can be withdrawn in the phone's settings.

6. Sharing

When you share your stack or your cupboard, the list of your products is written into the link or QR code: anyone who receives it can see it. No server is involved.

With WaSupp+, the PDF export is created on the phone. You choose who to send it to (e-mail, message…): the app sends it to no one.

7. Legal bases

Searches you start (unknown product, shops, map): performance of the service you request (GDPR article 6.1.b).

Recall check: legitimate interest in warning you about a product recall (GDPR article 6.1.f).

Health data: it stays on the phone and the publisher does not receive it. You choose to enter it, and can delete it at any time.

Messages sent with “Report a problem”: answering your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Personalised ads and access to the advertising identifier: your consent (GDPR article 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time.

Non-personalised ads: legitimate interest in funding the free app (GDPR article 6.1.f), or your consent when the Google form asks for it.

8. Retention period

On the phone: until you delete it (“Delete everything” in Profile, or by uninstalling the app).

Responses from online services: kept on the phone to avoid repeating the same request.

The publisher only receives messages sent with “Report a problem”: kept while the request is handled, one year at most, then deleted.

Data received by Google for ads: kept according to Google's rules.

9. Transfers outside the European Union

Nominatim is operated from the United Kingdom, a country recognised by the European Union as providing adequate protection. Apple and Google apply their own rules.

Web3Forms is operated from India: a message sent directly with “Report a problem” leaves the European Union. To avoid this, choose “Send with Mail”.

Google may process ad data in the United States, under the Data Privacy Framework between the European Union and the United States.

10. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, objection, portability: since your data is on your phone, you view, change and delete it directly in the app.

For what a third-party service receives (IP address, request), contact that service.

Ads: Settings › Ad privacy choices, and the phone's settings (tracking, advertising identifier).

For any question, write to the contact address. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country, for example the CNIL in France or the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands.

11. Minors

WaSupp is intended for people aged 18 and over.
